Name: Electrolyte

Yield: 50 servings (161.05g total, ~3.22g per serving)

Ingredients:
 - Morton salt (127g)
 - BulkSupplements potassium chloride (19.05g)
 - Nutricost magnesium malate (15.0g)
 - Water (24 fl oz / ~710mL per serving)

Directions:
 1. Weigh the dry ingredients and mix thoroughly
 2. Divide into 50 containers (~3.22g each), or store in an airtight container and mix again before portioning
 3. Add one serving to 24 fl oz (~710mL) of water and stir or shake well
 4. Adjust water to taste within 16-32 fl oz (~475-950mL) per serving

Notes:
 - 24 fl oz is a starting point within LMNT's suggested 16-32 fl oz range
 - Water guidance: https://science.drinklmnt.com/did-you-know/hydrate-for-endurance-sports

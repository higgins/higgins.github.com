Name: Smoothie 1

Ingredients:
 - Yogurt (2 spoonfuls)
 - Banana (1/2)
 - Spinach (a bunch)
 - Oats (a bunch)
 - Berries (a bunch)
 - Chocolate + peanut butter protein powder (1 serving)
 - Water

Directions:
 1. Add all ingredients to a blender
 2. Blend until smooth, adding water to reach the desired consistency
